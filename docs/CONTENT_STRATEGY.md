# SEO and AEO content strategy

## Principles

- One article answers one real buyer question, links to one service page, and
  carries a named author (Navneet Prasad) and a real date.
- Original reasoning from building real products (Vexel AI, Dental Health,
  EverGreen). No invented statistics; any external fact gets a source link.
- No prices in articles until the pricing rule changes (after 10 projects).
  Cost articles explain cost **drivers** instead.
- Quality over count: 2 articles a month is the ceiling for one author doing
  them properly.

## AEO on-page pattern (already applied to service pages)

- "What is X?" section with a 2 to 4 sentence definition.
- The problem, how we solve it, who it is for.
- Deliverables and timeline.
- FAQ with a cost-factor answer, emitted as FAQPage JSON-LD.
- Related live build where one genuinely exists.

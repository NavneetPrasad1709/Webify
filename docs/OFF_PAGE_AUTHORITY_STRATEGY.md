# Off-page authority strategy

A new domain with no clients earns authority slowly. These are the legitimate
moves, in order of payoff for Webify specifically.

| # | Activity | Why it works | Effort | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Founder LinkedIn profile + weekly posts about real builds | AI answers and buyers both look for a real person behind a company | 2 h/week | Profile views, inbound DMs |
| 2 | Verified directory profiles: Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Sortlist (fields ready in `brand-kit/listings/LISTINGS.md`) | These directories are frequently cited in "best agency" answers | 1 h each, once | Profile live, referral sessions |
| 3 | Google Business Profile (Greater Noida, real address) | Only fast local visibility route; must use the real address | 1 h + verification | GBP views, calls |
| 4 | First client case study + testimonial with the client's permission and public profile link | The single strongest trust signal the site lacks | After first project | Case-study page views |
| 5 | Open-source a small useful piece from a build (e.g. a Next.js starter or a component) on GitHub | Earns natural links from developers | 1 to 2 days | Stars, referring domains |
| 6 | Answer real questions on Reddit (r/SaaS, r/startups), Indie Hackers, Stack Overflow, without linking every time | Builds name recognition in places AI tools read | 1 h/week | Mentions, referral traffic |
| 7 | Guest technical posts on dev.to, Hashnode, or partner agency blogs | Real editorial links | 1 post/month | Referring domains |
| 8 | White-label partnerships with design studios (see brand-kit/outreach) | Revenue first, links and referrals second | Ongoing | Partner count |

## Not doing

Bought links, PBNs, link exchanges, comment spam, mass directory submission,
fake reviews, fake guest-post sites. Each risks a manual penalty or simply
wastes time.

## Measuring authority

Monthly: referring domains (Bing Webmaster Tools > Backlinks, free), branded
search impressions for "webify" (Search Console), directory profile views, and
whether Webify appears in the AI visibility test set (AI_VISIBILITY_TRACKER.md).

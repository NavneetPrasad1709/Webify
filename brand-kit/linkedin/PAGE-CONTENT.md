# LinkedIn company page: fields to paste

Everything below is ready to copy. Nothing here claims anything Webify cannot
show, which matters because the first thing a prospect does after reading a
cold message is open the page and look for the gap between the pitch and the
proof.

## Before you start

LinkedIn will ask for an email address **on the company domain** to verify the
page. Free Gmail and Outlook addresses are rejected. Every address on
webify.org.in currently hard bounces (`550 5.1.1`), so set up email hosting
first or the page cannot be verified. Zoho Mail's free tier covers one mailbox
on a custom domain and takes about twenty minutes.

Your personal profile also has to be in reasonable standing: more than one
connection, a filled-in profile, and a current position at Webify listed on it.
Add the position **before** creating the page.

**Company size is a decision, not a field.** The site says "team" on eleven
surfaces and names one person. Pick the answer you can defend on a call and
make the site agree with it. "1 employee" plus a named founder converts better
than an unverifiable "2-10", because a prospect who finds one person behind a
plural claim stops believing the rest of the page.

## First three posts

Post from your **personal profile** and reshare to the page. Personal reach on
LinkedIn is many times page reach, and a page with no followers has no
distribution at all on day one.

**1. The teardown.** Pick one of the three live builds and write what was hard
about it and what you decided. Screenshot, three paragraphs, link at the end.
This is the post that proves you can think, which is the only thing a stranger
is trying to work out.

**2. The number.** "This site loads X kB of JavaScript. Here is what came out
and why." You have the real before and after figures in AUDIT.md. Specific
numbers travel further than opinions.

**3. The offer.** Founding-client terms, stated plainly, with what you want in
return: an honest testimonial and permission to show the work. No countdown, no
scarcity theatre.

## What this page will and will not do

It will not bring clients. Nobody browses company pages. It is the thing a
prospect opens **after** a message, a proposal or a profile has already
interested them, and its whole job is to survive that check.

The client comes from the personal profile and from outbound. Set the page up
once, properly, in an hour, then spend the rest of the week on the two things
that actually produce conversations.
